# ADR-XXX-short-topic

## Status

Proposed

## Context

Describe the decision pressure, current constraints, and why the team needs a
decision now.

## Decision

State the chosen direction clearly and concretely.

## Alternatives Considered

List real alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Trade-Offs

Describe what the decision improves and what it makes harder, slower, riskier,
or more constrained.

## Consequences

List immediate effects on architecture, delivery, or verification.

## Reevaluation Triggers

List the conditions that would justify revisiting this decision.

## Notes

- When superseding an accepted ADR, create a new numbered ADR.
- Update the old ADR only to note `Superseded by ADR-XXX`.
- Do not rewrite the original decision history.
